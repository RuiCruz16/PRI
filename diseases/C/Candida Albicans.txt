A note from Cleveland Clinic
----------------------------
Many different harmless types of fungi live in and on your body, including Candida albicans. But sometimes, yeast multiplies uncontrollably, which can cause a fungal infection. If you develop any symptoms of an infection, talk to your healthcare provider. They’ll ask you questions about your symptoms and your medical history. If you have a fungal infection, they’ll prescribe antifungal medicine. Getting prompt treatment will relieve your discomfort and help you avoid potential complications.
